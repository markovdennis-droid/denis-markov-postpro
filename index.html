Planning your post-production

A few useful
answers.

What post-production services do you provide?

We bring together editing, colour grading and DI, sound editing and mixing, music, VFX, motion graphics, mastering and DCP creation. Subtitling, format conversion and post-production coordination can be included in the agreed scope.

Can we work with your team remotely?

Yes. Our freelance community works remotely with production companies, agencies and filmmakers worldwide. We agree media exchange, review stages and delivery requirements at the start of each project.

Can you handle one stage or the complete post-production?

Both. We can join an existing production for a specific discipline, or coordinate the workflow from ingest and editing through picture finishing, sound and final delivery. The specialists are selected around the needs of the project.

What do you need to scope a project?

Send a short brief, the type and approximate length of the film, your current production stage, the services you need, the delivery date and required formats. Reference material and a budget range help us suggest an appropriate team and workflow.

Do you work on films, commercials and television?

Yes. Our services cover film, advertising and television projects. The workflow and final deliverables are agreed for each project, whether that means a cinema DCP, broadcast master or versions for online release.
